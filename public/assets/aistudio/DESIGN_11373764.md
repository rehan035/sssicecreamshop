## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an artisanal, elevated confectionery experience. The aesthetic balances warmth, culinary craftsmanship, and luxurious indulgence while maintaining modern digital utility. It avoids playful juvenile tropes often associated with frozen treats, instead adopting the tactile, editorial poise of high-end culinary magazines and specialty patisseries.

### Emotional Response & Personality
- **Appetite & Indulgence:** Rich visual depth anchored by tones of roasted cocoa, citrus zest, and heavy churned cream.
- **Craft & Heritage:** Intentional typographic pairings that honor heritage European gelaterias alongside sharp contemporary ergonomics.
- **Warm Hospitality:** Inviting, legible, and unhurried layouts that evoke leisurely boutique tasting rooms.

### Design Movement
**Refined Tactile Editorial:** A hybrid of modern editorial publication structure and subtle tactile cues. The interface relies on warm layered surfaces, generous white (cream) space, crisp micro-borders, and deep, cocoa-tinted ambient shadows to suggest material substance without relying on glossy or skeuomorphic gimmickry.

## Colors

The palette directly references gourmet ingredients: bittersweet chocolate, candied blood orange, rich honeycomb, and dairy cream. The application strictly follows an intentional color hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#321C17` - Deep Cocoa):** Grounding structural element. Used for dominant typography, structural framing, high-contrast badges, and primary action buttons.
- **Secondary (`#F36B21` - Spiced Orange):** Vivid brand driver. Used for conversion-focused actions, promotional tags, active states, and focal UI highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#F5B832` - Honey Gold):** Accent layer. Dedicated to ratings, seasonal micro-badges, garnish highlights, and subtle warmth in iconography.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FFF6E9` - Clotted Cream):** Global foundation. Used as the dominant canvas backdrop instead of sterile cold white, lending an immediate rich, organic warmth to the viewport.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level elevation surface for interactive cards, sheets, and popovers to provide luminous separation above the cream canvas.
- **Accent Green (`#238A55` - Pistachio / Mint Infusion):** Botanical complement. Strictly reserved for dietary certifications (vegan, organic, gluten-free), ingredient provenance indicators, and positive purchase/freshness confirmations.

## Typography

The typography leverages an intentional tension between timeless editorial prestige and precision ergonomic readability.

- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Editorial Callouts):** Adds historic craft, high contrast, and tactile warmth. Used for product names, flavor hero titles, and storytelling pull quotes.
- **Manrope (Body, UI, & Pricing):** Provides an open, geometric, and functional counterweight to the display serif. Its modern construction ensures exceptional legibility across dense ingredient listings, nutritional facts, and compact digital ordering menus.
- **Typographic Treatment:** Uppercase styling is reserved strictly for `label-sm` and `label-md` when indicating dietary tags, scoop counts, or flavor categories, paired with loose letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for elevated sophistication.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column grid system (desktop) collapsing to an 8-column layout (tablet) and a 4-column layout (mobile).

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fluid container capped at 1320px maximum width. Features generous outer margins (`margin`: `3rem`) and a relaxed gutter (`gutter`: `1.5rem`), allowing high-resolution product photography to breathe alongside editorial flavor narratives.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Adapts to an 8-column model. Content cards re-orient into 2-column tasting flight configurations with balanced internal density.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column grid with compact outer margins (`margin-mobile`: `1.25rem`) and tighter gutters (`gutter-mobile`: `1rem`). Sticky transactional bars and scrollable horizontal carousels (flavor rails) bypass margins to preserve browsing velocity.
- **Spacing Cadence:** Vertical spacing strictly adheres to an 8px scale. Component interiors use `space-md` (`1rem`) to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) to maintain an uncluttered culinary presentation.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids sterile drop shadows and synthetic glassmorphism, instead using warmth-tinted ambient depth and tonal layer stacking.

- **Canvas Foundation:** Layer 0 sits at `#FFF6E9` (Warm Cream).
- **Surface Elevation:** Floating containers and cards sit at Layer 1 (`#FFFFFF`), outlined with a whisper-light cocoa boundary: `1px solid rgba(50, 28, 23, 0.08)`.
- **Warm Ambient Shadow:** Shadows mimic warm, directional confectionery shop lighting rather than synthetic black blur:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(50, 28, 23, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(50, 28, 23, 0.03)`
  - *Interactive Hover:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(50, 28, 23, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(50, 28, 23, 0.04)`
  - *Floating Menu / Modal:* `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(50, 28, 23, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(50, 28, 23, 0.06)`
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Surfaces rely on contrast against the clotted cream canvas rather than excessive vertical lift.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a calibrated roundedness level (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the smooth, sculptural curvature of scooped ice cream, poured ganache, and ceramic dessert bowls, without devolving into fully pill-shaped or toy-like aesthetics.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, dietary chips, buttons, and secondary badges.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px):** Applied to dessert product cards, menu item containers, tasting boards, and quick-add modals.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl` - 1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for hero display banners, featured flavor showcases, and bottom sheets on mobile viewports.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Strictly restricted to round counter controls (steppers) and floating status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Order / Checkout):** Deep Cocoa background (`#321C17`), pure white text, 8px radius, semi-bold Manrope. Hover shifts to subtle glow with 2px translateY lift.
- **Secondary Action (Flavor Details / Customize):** Premium Orange tint (`#F36B21`) with white text, or cream base with 1.5px Cocoa outline (`#321C17`).
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, Cocoa text, underline animation starting from center.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Ingredient Provenance / Dietary (Pistachio Green):** Soft green background (`rgba(35, 138, 85, 0.10)`), `#238A55` text, 4px vertical / 10px horizontal padding, 6px radius.
- **Flavor Notes & Attributes:** Warm Cream base with subtle `#321C17` hairline stroke, all-caps `label-sm` tracking.

### Product & Flavor Cards
- Pure White background (`#FFFFFF`) with 16px radius (`rounded-lg`).
- High-contrast visual header framing the dessert photography against warm backdrop.
- Playfair Display for item title, Manrope bold for unit price, and an inline Honey Gold (`#F5B832`) star accent for customer reviews.
- Resting border: `1px solid rgba(50, 28, 23, 0.08)`.

### Form Fields & Scoop Customizers
- **Input Fields:** Crisp White canvas, border tinted with `rgba(50, 28, 23, 0.20)`, 8px radius. Active focus transitions to a prominent 2px `#F36B21` stroke without blue OS halos.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Cocoa frames (`#321C17`) with solid Orange fill (`#F36B21`) and white indicator icon when selected.

### Flavor Stepper (Quantity & Portion Selector)
- Segmented pill container with `#FFF6E9` background.
- Crisp circular decrement/increment buttons with Cocoa icons and tactile active press depression.
